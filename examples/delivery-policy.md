# Invented demo policy: Delivery and missing tracking
Version: demo-1. Effective: 25 November 2026. Applies only to the invented demo store.

A draft order is a working record, not confirmation of a completed purchase. If payment status and fulfilment status are absent, say they are not recorded. Ask staff to confirm checkout and dispatch before giving a customer a shipping update.

An order marked unfulfilled has not been confirmed as shipped. If there is no tracking number or carrier event, staff must say shipping is not yet confirmed. Never promise a delivery date or claim a delay reason without supporting records.

For a paid, unfulfilled order, ask the fulfilment team to confirm dispatch status. Prepare an internal response explaining the observed status and next step. A draft must be reviewed by staff before any separate communication process.

A tracking number alone is not proof of delivery. Check recorded carrier events before claiming a parcel has arrived. This demo cannot send messages, issue refunds, or change orders.
